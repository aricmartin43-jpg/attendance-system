# Cosmos Tamil safety awareness videos

These narrated cartoon explainers cover the core Cosmos workshop and support roles. Each module has simple Tamil narration, matching large Tamil text, illustrated scenes with gentle motion and transitions, a readable transcript, a separate 10-question quiz, and a supervisor practical-review record in the portal. These are illustrated explainer videos, not character-rigged cartoons or complete machine-operation courses.

Review each module against the actual factory, equipment manufacturer instructions, chemical safety data sheets, emergency arrangements and approved work procedure before employee rollout. Verify spoken technical terminology with a Tamil-speaking supervisor. No quiz or practical record grants equipment authorisation.

## Coverage

- Arc welding / fabrication awareness (TIG, MIG and ARC need their own approved machine-specific instruction).
- Enclosed laser cutting awareness.
- Press brake bending awareness.
- Grinding / finishing awareness.
- Sheet metal material handling awareness; crane and forklift operation need separate training.
- Covers / conveyors assembly and quality inspection awareness.
- Maintenance / field service and energy isolation awareness.
- Painting / powder coating awareness; chemical-specific controls must be confirmed.
- Stores / purchasing / dispatch awareness.
- Design / administration / managers / workshop visitors awareness.
- Drilling / rotating-tool awareness.
- Resistance spot welding awareness.

Specialist tasks, including lifting-equipment operation, live electrical work, work at height and confined-space entry, require separate training and permits. This collection does not teach their operation.

## Assessments

Each selected module is scored on the server using its own question bank. Pass mark is 8/10. Incorrect answers receive explanations after submission. Attempt history retains the lesson ID. Employees see their own records; administrators can record an observed practical review for a passing attempt. An employee cannot approve their own practical review.

## Production notes

720p H.264 / AAC MP4, Tamil synthetic narration (`ta-IN-ValluvarNeural`), gentle image motion and fade transitions, large Tamil captions. The portal uses `preload="none"` and manual playback to avoid downloading all videos. Poster images come from original generated cartoon storyboards. No images were copied from third-party safety material.

Image briefs: (1) six distinct safe welding scenes with consistent South Indian adult workers, PPE, fume extraction, screening, damaged cable isolation and hot-work zone; the damaged-cable panel was revised to show no touching. (2) nine distinct safe-role scenes for enclosed laser, guarded press brake, stopped grinder inspection, trolley handling, stationary assembly, locked-off maintenance, ventilated paint booth, stable stores and workshop briefing. (3) stopped guarded drill press with clamped work and no gloves, and stopped guarded resistance spot welder. Clean modern blue/cyan editorial cartoon style, no text or branding inside the source illustrations. Built-in image generation was used for the storyboard illustrations.

Reference links are listed per lesson in `training.py` and `training_modules.json` and shown in the portal. They provide general awareness guidance; they are not an Indian legal compliance certification.
